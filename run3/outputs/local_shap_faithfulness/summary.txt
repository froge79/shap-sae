Local DeepSHAP vs per-example ablation
==================================================
target=lm  n=100  k_local=20  k_global=20

[A] All candidates
  mean Spearman ρ(|local φ| vs |Δ|)  = 0.246 ± 0.145
  mean Spearman ρ(|global Φ| vs |Δ|) = -0.150 ± 0.143
  fraction examples with ρ_local > ρ_global = 0.980

[B] Active candidates only (control)
    Globally-top features are usually inactive in any one sentence, and
    ablating an inactive feature gives Δ = 0 exactly, while local |φ(x)|
    tracks activity by construction. [B] removes that advantage.
  mean active candidates = 15.0 of 29.8
  mean Spearman ρ(|local φ| vs |Δ|)  = -0.155 ± 0.236
  mean Spearman ρ(|global Φ| vs |Δ|) = 0.155 ± 0.239
  fraction examples with ρ_local > ρ_global = 0.137

Local SHAP ranks per-example ablation effects better than global Φ → averaging was likely a main error source.
